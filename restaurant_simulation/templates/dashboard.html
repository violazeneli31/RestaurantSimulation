What the columns mean

Tickets flow left → right. Each waiter thread put()s items into station queues; chef threads get() them — this is the textbook producer–consumer pattern. Items only enter Cooking once a chef acquires the resources they need.

Kitchen Staff

one thread each

Why some chefs wait

Grill and Pasta chefs must acquire() a stove before they can cook — there are fewer stoves than chefs, so they compete. That's a threading.Semaphore. Dessert chefs don't need a stove and never wait.

How to read it

Every line is one synchronization event from a worker thread: ORDER (waiter submits), WAIT_STOVE / GOT_STOVE (semaphore), COOK → ITEM_DONE, then READY → SERVED (condition variable wakes delivery).
